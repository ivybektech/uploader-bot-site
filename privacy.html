1. Ilova nima qiladi

GS School 62 Uploader Bot (keyingi o‘rinlarda — «bot») — Telegram orqali yuborilgan videolarni kanal egasining YouTube kanaliga yuklaydigan ichki vosita. Bot faqat kanal egasi ruxsat bergan foydalanuvchilar uchun ishlaydi.

2. Google hisobidan qanday ma’lumot olinadi

Kanal egasi botga Google OAuth orqali quyidagi ruxsatlarni beradi:

  • YouTube’ga video yuklash (youtube.upload) — faqat foydalanuvchi botga yuborgan videolarni uning o‘z kanaliga joylash uchun;
  • YouTube ma’lumotlarini o‘qish (youtube.readonly) — kanal va bot yuklagan videolarning ko‘rishlar, yoqtirishlar va izohlar sonini ko‘rsatish uchun.

Bot Google hisobingizning boshqa ma’lumotlariga (pochta, kontaktlar, fayllar) kirmaydi.

3. Ma’lumotlar qanday ishlatiladi va saqlanadi

  • Video fayllar yuklash tugaguncha serverda vaqtincha saqlanadi va keyin o‘chiriladi.
  • Sarlavha va tavsiflar bot bazasida saqlanadi, chunki ular yuklangan videoning tarixi hisoblanadi.
  • Google tomonidan berilgan kirish tokeni serverda saqlanadi va faqat yuqoridagi maqsadlarda ishlatiladi.
  • Ma’lumotlar uchinchi shaxslarga sotilmaydi, berilmaydi va reklama uchun ishlatilmaydi.
  • Video nutqidan sarlavha yozish uchun videoning audio qismi Google Gemini xizmatiga yuboriladi; u faqat matn hosil qilish uchun ishlatiladi.

4. Google foydalanuvchi ma’lumotlari siyosatiga muvofiqlik

Botning Google API’lardan olingan ma’lumotlardan foydalanishi va ularni boshqa ilovalarga uzatishi Google API Services User Data Policy talablariga, shu jumladan «Limited Use» (cheklangan foydalanish) talablariga mos keladi.

5. Ruxsatni bekor qilish

Botning Google hisobingizga kirishini istalgan vaqtda myaccount.google.com/permissions sahifasida bekor qilishingiz mumkin. Shundan keyin bot kanalingizga yuklay olmaydi.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-22

1. What the application does

GS School 62 Uploader Bot (“the bot”) is an internal tool that uploads videos sent through Telegram to the channel owner’s YouTube channel. It works only for users approved by the channel owner.

2. Google account data we access

  • Upload YouTube videos (youtube.upload) — only to publish the videos the user sends to the bot, on the user’s own channel;
  • View YouTube data (youtube.readonly) — to show view, like and comment counts for the channel and the videos the bot uploaded.

The bot does not access any other Google account data (mail, contacts, files).

3. How data is used and stored

  • Video files are stored temporarily on our server until the upload completes and are deleted afterwards.
  • Titles and descriptions are kept in the bot’s database as the history of uploads.
  • The access token issued by Google is stored on our server and used only for the purposes above.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • To draft titles, the audio track of a video is sent to Google’s Gemini service and used only to generate text.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

The bot’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Revoking access

You can revoke the bot’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The bot will then no longer be able to upload to your channel.
