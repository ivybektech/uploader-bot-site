Video Uploader Bot

An internal tool for the GS School 62 team. Team members send short videos to a Telegram bot, which uploads them to the school’s own YouTube channel (Shorts), sets titles and schedules publishing.

The bot works only for accounts approved by the channel owner and accesses YouTube only through the permission the channel owner grants. It is not a public service.
